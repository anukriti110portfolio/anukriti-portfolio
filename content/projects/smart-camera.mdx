## Research

Reframed the problem through generative research with 12 users, a competitive
analysis of 10 products, and a causal analysis of what actually stops people
from taking good photos.

## Insights

Poor photography is an execution-under-constraint problem, not a knowledge
gap — people already know the basics of a good photo; they just can't apply
them in the moment.
