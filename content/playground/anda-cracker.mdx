<Section title="The “Anda” Ritual">

### Daily Staple

Eggs are the universal protein in Indian households from quick morning omelets to Sunday egg curries.

- High consumption volume across all demographics.
- Traditional methods rely on table-edges or spoons.
- Cooking is often a synchronous task (stirring with one hand while cracking with the other).

<Section title="The Shell Trap">

### Precision Failure

A “clean crack” is a culinary skill that many home cooks and beginners struggle with.

One imprecise strike leads to shell fragments entering the pan, creating a physical hazard and an unappetizing experience.

The Hazard: Fragments are hard to spot in whisked yolks and can carry surface contaminants into the food.

<Section title="The Peeling Frustration">

### Messy & Tedious

Peeling boiled eggs in the Indian context often involves fresh eggs which are notoriously difficult to peel without tearing the white.

- The membrane sticks to the shell.
- The result is a “mangled” egg that looks unappealing for serving.
- Process is time consuming for large family batches.

<Section title="The Design Brief">

<Lead>100 % Clean separation</Lead>

### Project Brief

To develop a handheld device that provides a controlled fracture for raw eggs and a membrane safe release for boiled eggs.

### Primary Users:

Home cooks, bachelor students, and large scale breakfast vendors (Rehri-walas).

<Section title="Engineering Limitations & Future Tuning">

**Spring stiffness (k):** A critical unknown; the spring must be strong enough to crack the shell but not so stiff that it crushes the entire egg.

**Energy Calibration:** Research suggests an impact energy of 12-26 mJ is ideal for a clean crack.

**he Formula:** We must solve for U= 1/2kx^2

- U = Required impact energy.
- k = Spring constant.
- x= Displacement (compression distance).

**Next Steps:** Testing with various spring gauges and adjustable compression slots to accommodate different egg sizes and shell thicknesses.

</Section>
