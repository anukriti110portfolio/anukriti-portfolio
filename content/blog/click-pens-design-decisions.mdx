<Section title="Click pens">

Study and unpack how design decisions were made and translated into manufacturable and usable objects in click pens. Submitted by Anukriti Tripathi.

</Section>

<Section title="How it all started?">

Losing pen caps always made me anxious. Caps get misplaced, ink dries, and using the pen repeatedly becomes inconvenient. I always preferred click pens because they felt faster and easier to use, but I never explored how such a small object contains so much engineering.

</Section>

<Section title="Evolution of pen mechanisms">

<Section title="Form development and ergonomics">

The cylindrical form of the pen gives:

- Natural finger fit
- Balanced weight distribution
- Pocket portability
- Continuous rotational symmetry for assembly

Ergonomic considerations include grip diameter, surface texture, click force, writing comfort and hand posture. The form is directly influenced by internal mechanism arrangement.

<Section title="Manufacturing process">

Processes used:

- Injection molding for the plastic body
- Metal machining for the tip
- Spring coiling
- Pad printing for branding
- Snap-fit assembly

The pen is designed for mass production, fast assembly, low manufacturing cost and minimal material usage.

<Section title="Structural & assembly decisions">

Structural decisions improve strength, assembly efficiency, durability and material optimization. Features:

- Snap fit joints reduce screws
- Cylindrical geometry improves strength
- Internal ribs support thin walls
- Modular refill system enables replacement

The embodiment balances durability with low cost manufacturing.

<Section title="Multi color pen as advanced embodiment">

Multi color pens increase embodiment complexity by integrating multiple refills, selector mechanisms, additional springs and a compact internal arrangement. The design demonstrates space optimization, mechanical coordination and increased usability in a compact form.

<Section title="Conclusion">

The click pen demonstrates how embodiment design transforms a simple concept into a manufacturable, ergonomic and mechanically functional product.

The study revealed how:

- User problems influence innovation
- Mechanisms shape form
- Materials affect usability
- Manufacturing constraints guide design decisions

Even everyday products contain sophisticated engineering and thoughtful design integration.

</Section>
