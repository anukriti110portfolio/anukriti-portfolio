Four routes exist. They differ in who processes your application, not in how important they are.

e-VisaProcessed online by the Bureau of Immigration

Apply online for short visits for tourism, business or medical reasons, if your country is eligible.

Apply / e-Visa

Apply for an e-Visa

Apply online for a short visit to India. You pay the fee online, receive an electronic travel authorization by email, and show a printout at immigration.

How it works

  1. 1Apply onlineFill in the form and upload your photo and passport page.
  2. 2Pay the fee onlineBy credit or debit card, or a payment wallet.
  3. 3Receive your ETAYour Electronic Travel Authorization is sent to your email.
  4. 4Fly to IndiaShow your printed ETA at immigration. The e-Visa is stamped on your passport.

Before you start

  • A passport valid for at least six months
  • A recent front-facing photo
    JPEG, 10 KB to 1 MB, at least 350 × 350 pixels, white or light background
  • A scan of your passport details page
    PDF format
  • A card or wallet to pay the fee online
  • Your arrival airport and expected arrival date
